1) Only edit solution.py. Keep design_molecules(spec) -> list of SMILES strings.
2) Deterministic CPU code. Python standard library, numpy, scipy and RDKit only. RDKit is
   available to you on purpose: you are expected to build and run your own search loop.
3) At most spec["max_submissions"] SMILES per call. Duplicates after canonicalization count once.
4) Molecules too similar to the approved-drug reference panel are discarded as recall, not design.
5) Seconds to a couple of minutes per evaluation. Do not read verification/ or frontier_eval/.

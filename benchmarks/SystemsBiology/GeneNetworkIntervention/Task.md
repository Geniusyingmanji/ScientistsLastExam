# GeneNetworkIntervention — discover a dynamic regulatory network and design a phenotype intervention

## Scientific background

Perturbational gene-expression measurements can distinguish regulatory mechanisms that are
indistinguishable in passive steady-state data. In this synthetic benchmark, four normalized
gene-expression states obey the public nonlinear model

```text
dx_j/dt = decay_j * (sigmoid(bias_j + sum_i (2*x_i - 1)*weight[i,j]
                                      + intervention_j) - x_j).
```

`weight[i,j] > 0` means gene `i` activates gene `j`; a negative value means repression.
Self-regulation is excluded. Most worlds contain a nontrivial network generated by this family.
Null-network worlds require refusing a regulatory-edge claim; latent-regulator worlds require
refusing the declared four-gene family. In both cases, reporting a nontrivial four-gene
mechanism is a false discovery.

## Your task

Implement:

```python
def discover_gene_network(gene_names, perturb, phenotype_objective, budget_units):
    """Return a dict containing:
      weights:       finite (n_genes,n_genes) source-by-target matrix
      support:       optional same-shape 0/1 matrix
      biases:        finite length-n_genes vector
      decay_rates:   positive finite length-n_genes vector
      intervention:  length-n_genes phenotype intervention
      confidence:    optional scalar in [0,1]
      abstain:       optional bool; True means no supported nontrivial-network claim

    perturb(intervention, n_steps) returns:
      time:          shape (n_steps + 1,)
      expression:    noisy normalized expression, shape (n_steps + 1,n_genes)
      intervention:  applied CRISPRi/a input, shape (n_steps,n_genes)
    """
```

An experiment may pass one constant intervention vector or an `(n_steps,n_genes)` schedule.
Negative values represent CRISPRi-like repression and positive values CRISPRa-like activation.
Inputs lie in `[-2,2]`, may target at most two genes, and use 20–80 time steps. A query costs
`ceil(n_steps/20) + number_of_targeted_genes` units. Exceeding `budget_units` invalidates the
world even if callback errors are caught.

`phenotype_objective` states the public parameter bounds and the intervention constraints. The
fourth gene is the protected phenotype readout and cannot be directly perturbed. Your final
intervention may target at most two of the first three genes.

## Evaluation

- `combined_score` is the development geometric joint of signed-network/parameter recovery,
  sealed trajectory prediction, and phenotype intervention utility, normalized above the
  always-abstain baseline.
- mechanism, prediction and decision utility are also reported separately.
- `robustness_score` replays the same submitted intervention under sealed intervention-
  efficiency, kinetic and regulatory-strength shifts.
- held-out topologies/noise, null/latent-regulator refusal, confidence calibration, budget and
  per-world outcomes remain evaluator-only.

The metrics are not experimental biological evidence. A good trajectory fit without a correct
signed mechanism or a transferable phenotype intervention is not a validated discovery.

# Screen a virtual science world

Read the target repository's env/CONTRACT.md and env/AUTHORING.md before choosing integration points. Inventory existing worlds: a new wrapper or renamed state variable is not a new scientific challenge.

Start from observed behavior in scientific trajectories. Separate prediction, quantitative claim calibration, mechanism discrimination and scope testing. Tag API failures, truncated replies, invalid programs and schema confusion separately; none establishes scientific difficulty. High scores on assigned values or known null effects do not establish mechanism discovery. Successful prediction can coexist with a wrong explanation.

For each candidate, specify a concrete pair of competing accounts, the restricted observations under which they agree, and a legal experiment that can separate them. Include at least one genuinely unresolved regime. State the observable claim supported by that experiment without promising unique internal structure recovery. Prefer tests of intervention, temporal ordering or extrapolation over asking the candidate to name an author's hidden label.

Rank candidates by distinct scientific leverage and implementability. Record rejections, including duplicates of existing kernels, unavailable interventions, prohibitively expensive simulation, or nonidentifiability. A low baseline score alone is not a reason to accept a world.

Before development runs, freeze a small local plan: instances, experiment and analysis budgets, numerical limits, control ranges, noise model, panel split and stopping condition. These development instances are exposed and must not later be described as unseen evaluation instances. Structural holdout requires a generator split, not only a new seed.

Use the shared World interface with a separate public apparatus contract and private kernel. Describe units, reset semantics, observation noise and legal manipulations clearly; hide sampled mechanisms, parameters and future panels. A clean operator run must not become a candidate action. Keep an incomplete integration unregistered and label it prototype; do not silently fall back to another world's claim or noise adapters.

Validate with an independent numerical method or analytic limit, invariants, extreme legal controls, noise and numerical-error separation, and one concrete ambiguity/discrimination witness. Report actual work consumed. Compare no-observation and public-data baselines with an author-informed reference under an explicit budget. Label an oracle's privileges: known family, parameter bounds and fixed design are stronger priors than a candidate receives. Oracle success establishes only that scoped problem's feasibility, not agent discoverability or universal identifiability.

Design open evidence outputs: frozen executable predictions, new observations, revisions and scoped falsifiable claims. Permit both rivals to fail and permit inconclusive outcomes. Do not grade free-form science by exact author wording. Keep numeric accuracy separate from manual discovery-depth evidence; do not replace historical scores with prospective criteria.

Deliver the candidate decision record, implementation status, local validation evidence and unresolved limitations. Release only aggregate readiness information. Keep sampled parameters, sealed panels, raw trajectories and credentials out of public reports. Public source can become training material; do not claim contamination resistance from randomized coefficients or renamed variables alone.

Paid model calls, retries and campaign expansion require an explicit user budget. Local implementation authorization does not authorize those calls. Preserve frozen cohorts and their denominators.

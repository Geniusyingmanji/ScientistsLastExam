# Research findings — new environment pilot

## Original prospective workflow (2026-10-03)

Thirty GPT-5.6-sol episodes used 138 requests: six completed, 18 ended on invalid
actions and six on network transport. All 30 scientific receipt chains verified;
20 stored test verdicts replayed. Seven tests yielded scoped predictive adequacy,
11 were inconclusive and two had headline refutation outcomes. At candidate
level, three tests refuted four candidate instances, including one candidate in
an otherwise inconclusive comparison. One both-refuted result involved a
predictor dimensional error, not clean elimination of two scientific mechanisms.

All 11 inconclusive tests had a predeclared uncertainty radius larger than every
candidate tolerance; adequacy was unreachable, though refutation remained
possible. For ecology, the current conservative radius is at least
0.08558165 × total absolute readout weight, above the non-wide tolerance ceiling
0.05 × that weight. Strict two-candidate discrimination is therefore unreachable
with the current replication/experiment limits. This must be calibrated in a
future protocol, without rewriting these frozen results.

The independent secondary result-to-claim review supports only the narrow claim
that the apparatus permits open questions, sealed new-condition tests and some
scoped evidence/counterexamples. It does not support general scientific-success
rates, automatic DiscoveryDepth, whole-mechanism validation or five-world
scientific success. Review route: gpt-6-astra / ultra, same-family, provisional,
pending external review. No EXPERIMENT_AUDIT.json was available. The canonical
evidence_check.py was unresolved; actual raw records and arithmetic were read
and independently checked instead. Full private traces are in .aris and operator
artifacts, not committed.

## Separate interface-repair cohort

The frozen v0.3 supplement uses 30 new episodes, 19 requests each at most, within
the same 720-request ledger. It discloses interface limits/statistical planning
and permits public schema corrections before dispatch. Scientific rules remain
unchanged. New instances, prompts and request caps prevent causal improvement
claims. The repaired cohort closed with 29/30 submitted reports and one terminal revised
predictor failure. It consumed 401 requests, bringing both cohorts to 539/720.
All 30 repaired science receipt chains and 70 completed numerical verdicts
replayed exactly; 1,878 observation keys were unique across the repaired cohort.
The public narrative export was cross-checked against 30 raw reports, 401 steps,
189 analyses, 1,878 observation summaries and 70 sealed tests, with no substantive
field differences under the documented redaction rules.

The 70 test outcomes were 50 scoped adequacy, four scoped discrimination,
nine headline refutations and seven inconclusive. Fifteen tests refuted
18 candidate instances. Twenty-two schema rejections occurred in 12 episodes;
21 were followed by subsequent science and one by finish. All 12 affected
episodes eventually submitted reports. These process counts do not establish
scientific-success rates or causal benefit from the repair.

The strongest controlled findings concern catalyst pretreatment severity beyond
reaction count in two independent instances. Other examples include climate
history effects, comparison of fitted response-time models, molecular geometric
transfer counterexamples, and phase-spectrum counterexample-driven revisions.
These support the stated tests' scope. They do not identify unique hidden
mechanisms, complete phase diagrams or full force fields. All 13 ecology
adequacy results use wide tolerances; the strict-discrimination ceiling persists.

Future work should first calibrate attainable uncertainty and strong scientific
baselines, then combine self-selected discoveries with independent coverage
checks. Scaling should use fixed protocols and paired instance budgets after
these checks, rather than interpreting more selected scalar passes as depth.


The final independent continuation review returned `claim_supported: yes` only
for the narrow apparatus/process/evidence claim, with high confidence in that
restricted reading. Route: `CONFIRM_NARROW_CLAIM_ONLY`; no further runs are needed
for that claim. The reviewer independently checked 30 manifests, 60 journals,
70 raw means/radii/flags and their sealed program/prediction bindings, 401 requests,
and 15 fresh instance clusters without original-instance overlap. This remains
same-family/provisional, pending external review; integrity audit status is
unavailable. The final trace is the second review in the local result-to-claim
run; private reviewer paths and unredacted artifacts are not published.

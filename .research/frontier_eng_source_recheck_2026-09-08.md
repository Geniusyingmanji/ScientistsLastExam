## Focused problem-class findings

These findings concern the current engineering tasks and the separate PFSP submission; applicability depends on which tasks a PR contains. They do not imply that withdrawn tasks are present.

- **PermutationFlowShop — same-class overlap remains; the current same-problem-class admission gate is not satisfied.** FE [JobShop/abz](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/JobShop/abz/Task.md) and [JobShop/ta](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/JobShop/ta/Task.md) minimize makespan subject to job precedence and machine capacity. PFSP imposes a common machine route and a common job permutation across machines: it is a restricted scheduling subclass with the same objective. Its instances and output interface differ, but fresh seeds, NEH-based scaling, stronger ILS, and faster evaluation do not remove the same-class relationship. Admission requires an explicit maintainer decision on whether that subclass is permitted; absent that decision it remains unpassed.
- **CompositeLaminateStacking — related structural-design domain, with substantive problem differences.** SLE reorders a fixed ply-angle multiset, subject to symmetry, balance, and manufacturing constraints, to improve buckling/first-ply reserve. FE [ISCSO2015](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/StructuralOptimization/ISCSO2015/Task.md) and [ISCSO2023](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/StructuralOptimization/ISCSO2023/Task.md) choose truss sections/geometry to reduce weight; [TopologyOptimization](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/StructuralOptimization/TopologyOptimization/Task.md) and [EngDesign/YJ_02](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/EngDesign/YJ_02/LLM_prompt.txt) distribute material to reduce compliance; [YJ_03](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/EngDesign/YJ_03/LLM_prompt.txt) targets crack-tip stress intensity. These are concrete differences in variables, mechanics, and objectives, not just new seeds. “Same form, different scientific problem” is defensible at that granularity, subject to maintainer acceptance; no independent laminate-model validation is claimed.
- **WakeAwareFarmCoDesign — no matching scientific task found in the checked catalogs and nearest-neighbor contracts.** SLE jointly selects static turbine locations and directional yaw to optimize wake-coupled farm energy/value. FE [UAVInspectionCoverageWithWind](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/Robotics/UAVInspectionCoverageWithWind/Task.md) chooses time-dependent flight controls for inspection coverage and energy under wind disturbance; [DawnAircraftDesignOptimization](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier-Engineering/blob/e3fa29c193356af2ce1ec8b3d23ab1a2e2410071/benchmarks/Aerodynamics/DawnAircraftDesignOptimization/Task.md) minimizes aircraft mass through seven continuous system-design variables. Neither optimizes inter-turbine wakes or farm yield. Sharing wind/aerodynamics does not make these the same problem. This finding does not validate SLE's reduced wake model against FLORIS or field data.

The source discrepancy and problem-class decisions are explicit open items, not implied approvals. Existing task cards and reports must retain their pending status until the corresponding maintainer decisions and required validation are recorded.

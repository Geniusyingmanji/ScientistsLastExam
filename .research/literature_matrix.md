## Synthesis for Frontier-Science

The literature separates at least four capabilities that should not be collapsed into one score:

1. **Optimization** — find a high-quality feasible artifact under an oracle budget.
2. **Learning from feedback** — outperform no-feedback, shuffled-feedback, random-search and classical optimizer controls across repeated runs.
3. **Scientific validity** — recover mechanisms, survive hidden shifts/interventions and high-fidelity confirmation, and abstain under misspecification.
4. **Research integrity** — preserve provenance, failed branches, hypothesis/evidence updates, costs and re-executable artifacts.

EdgeBench strengthens the case for treating long-horizon improvement as a trajectory rather
than an endpoint, but it does not collapse these four capabilities. In particular, accumulated
workspace/context can beat independent restarts even if a system is exploiting visible proxies
rather than learning a valid scientific mechanism. Feedback-causal controls and sealed
validation therefore remain separate requirements.

The current repository implements the secure evaluation and protocol infrastructure for (1),
including baseline-only integration smokes for three official search frameworks, but still lacks
valid multi-seed nonzero-budget evidence. Its strongest defensible near-term contribution is
therefore a benchmark for **cross-domain, executable, budget-constrained scientific generative
optimization**, with later “discovery” claims gated on (2)–(4).

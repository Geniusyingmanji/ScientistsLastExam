## Scientific contract and repairs

The task asks for the causal typed variants and signed per-allele effects at one synthetic
GWAS locus, or a refusal when a stipulated finite-sample RSS configuration gap is too small.
The nuisance is cohort/reference-panel LD mismatch; the experimental action buys at most six
exact in-cohort LD rows among sixty variants. This differs from site-frequency-spectrum
population-history inversion and fixed-library marker-mixture assignment.

The original truth divided generating effects by `sqrt(Var(signal) + 1)` even though the
accepted finite sample phenotype was standardized by its realised standard deviation. The
oracle now uses that same realised scale for truth. A separate regression test verifies the
units, and individual-level ordinary least squares independently verifies the complete
reference's marginal-t-to-joint-effect conversion. Reusing centered-genotype sufficient
statistics changes the marginal-regression implementation, not its estimand; a separate
intercept-inclusive least-squares recomputation checks three phenotypes and seven variants.

The original reference intentionally omitted its documented local masked-partner test.
That method is now an omission probe. The admission reference includes that test, charged
residual/resolution-row acquisition, ambiguity refusal, and exact joint effect conversion.
It is a standalone candidate with no oracle, simulator, truth or source imports. Its remaining
effect error is sampling error; no standard competence is disabled to lower the threshold.

RSS is a working approximation to the marginal regression summaries. These curated accepted
worlds are a finite-sample challenge, not an estimate of the frequency of such loci in real
GWAS or a claim of non-identifiability with unlimited data. Primary background sources are
[Zhu and Stephens, RSS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5796536/) and
[Benner et al., reference LD and fine-mapping](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5630179/).
No unresolved scientific contradiction was found within this stated operational model;
external statistical-genetics review remains pending.

Development and heldout mechanism, false-verdict rate, refusal and attempted-discovery
coverage each retain explicit numeric numerators and denominators. False-discovery here means
false scientific verdicts per world, including an unwarranted ambiguity verdict; it is not
claim-conditioned FDR. Invalid worlds remain in their planned denominators and receive no
refusal credit. The public score is normalized above blanket refusal, which is exactly zero.

The wrapper uses the shared fail-closed launcher, with an explicit 300-second timeout and
private full-metric sidecars. Each scientific world resets the supported candidate session
outside the invalid-submission handler. All LD callbacks within that world keep their process
and tmpfs, while module globals, imported state and tmpfs cannot cross the next world or the
development/heldout boundary. A real CandidateProxy regression checks every one of 18 worlds,
repeat rows costing one unit, and preserved state within each world; a separate test requires
failed session establishment to escape scientific scoring.

## Admission limits and next gate

No model was called by this review. A new fixed first-proposal calibration on the final task
package is still required; prior builder numbers, synthetic unit tests and the contribution
gate cannot establish model difficulty. Top-level `valid=1` means at least one development
world was valid under the existing multi-world evaluator contract. A calibration candidate
therefore needs `feasibility_rate=1` **and every one of 18 per-instance rows valid** before its
score can support difficulty. An invalid or partial proposal is a protocol result, not evidence
that the scientific task defeated the model. Do not redraw to replace an unsuccessful sample.

Global inventory/frozen-evidence updates remain the maintainer's responsibility. This review
changes no historical run manifests and does not promote the task from candidate status.
